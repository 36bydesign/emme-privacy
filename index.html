Emme Privacy Policy

Emme - Baby Tracker · Last updated October 6, 2026

The short version. Emme has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. What you log lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, where we cannot read it. If you share your baby with a partner, a small relay we run passes along timer updates and notifications between your two phones. We do not sell or share anything, and we do not track you.

What you log

Feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth, medicines, vaccines, milestones, notes, photos, and your baby's name, birth date, and sex are stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). They count against your own iCloud storage. We have no access to this data.

When you share your baby with a partner, Apple's iCloud sharing gives that person access to the same entries. You choose who is invited, and you can stop sharing at any time in Settings → Sharing.

The relay for partner updates

If you share with a partner, Emme uses a small relay server we run (hosted on Cloudflare) so that a timer started on one phone can appear on the other phone's Lock Screen, and so your partner can get a quiet banner when you log an entry. The relay is not used when you are not sharing.

The relay keeps:

  • Each phone's push notification tokens and a random ID for that install of the app.
  • The latest status for the shared baby: the baby's name, any running timer, and the times of the last feed and diaper.

It keeps no history. Entry banners (for example "Wet diaper · 3:07 PM") are passed to Apple's push notification service for delivery and are not stored by us. A phone that has not connected for 60 days is forgotten automatically. The relay does not know your name, email address, or Apple Account, and the baby is identified only by a scrambled code.

Location

If you allow it, Emme uses your approximate location to work out sunrise and sunset, so its sky can follow the real day. The location is rounded to about 7 miles, stays on your devices, and is never sent to us. This is optional, and you can turn it off in the iOS Settings app.

Photos

Emme only sees the photos you pick. They are stored with your entries on your device and in your iCloud.

Summaries

The optional written summaries are made on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. Nothing is sent to us or to any other company to create them.

Export files

Settings → Export data makes a file with your baby's entries and that phone's settings. The file goes only where you save or send it.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with other companies.
  • No accounts, so we hold no email addresses or passwords.

TestFlight

If you install a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, and makes them available to us so we can fix problems. Apple's own privacy policy covers that.

Children

Emme is a tool for parents and caregivers. It is not meant to be used by children. The information about a baby is entered by the adults caring for that baby and is handled as described above.

Deleting your data

  • Entries: Settings → Clear all data removes every entry from your phones and from iCloud.
  • The app: deleting Emme removes its data from that device. You can remove its iCloud data in the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • The relay: turn off sharing or stop using the app and the relay forgets your phone within 60 days. Email us if you want it removed sooner.

Changes
